General scheduling rules

  • NP = new patient: totally new to the clinic or not seen in 3 years.
  • Only 1 NP per time slot, and no NPs in the last hour of the clinic (longer appts).
  • Any slot with any provider works — no matching by insurance or clinical presentation.
  • Check for an upcoming encounter first: if it is more than 3 months away (or the referral is STAT/Urgent), continue scheduling.
  • Always double-check the location on community clinic appointments.
  • Provider doesn't practice at the 3 closest locations? Use Google Maps to find the closest location they do practice at.
  • No provider specified for a new patient: Mohamed, Alkawaleet, Gedela, or Boppana.

Urgent / STAT / ASAP

  • No availability within ~5 business days → Tel Enc to Referrals,Staff.
  • Do not schedule within 48 hours (needs nurse approval).
  • Scheduling within 48–72 hours → Tel Enc to the provider's staff to prep the chart.
  • Nothing for 2 months with a specific provider/clinic: may break double/triple-booking rules unless the block max is reached (still 1 NP per slot, no NP in last hour).
